SaveIt Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

SaveIt is a personal-use tool that lets a user save places (restaurants, cafes, and similar) from social content shared over Instagram Direct Message. This page explains what data SaveIt collects and how it is used.

What we collect

When you send a Direct Message to the SaveIt Instagram account, we receive and process:

  • The text of your message, if any
  • The URL of any shared post, reel, or link included in the message
  • A thumbnail image associated with a shared post, when Instagram provides one
  • Your Instagram-scoped ID (an identifier tied to this app and your Instagram account — not your username, email, or phone number)
  • Message metadata such as timestamp and message ID, used only to avoid processing the same message twice

We do not have access to, and do not attempt to access, the caption or metadata of posts made by other Instagram accounts. We only see what Instagram includes directly in the webhook event for your message.

How we use it

Message content is used solely to identify the place referenced in a shared post:

  • Message text and any attached thumbnail image are analyzed (including with an AI model) to extract signals like a place name or location
  • Those signals are used to look up a matching place via the Google Places API
  • The resulting place is stored so it can be shown back to you in the SaveIt app

Saved places are associated with your Instagram-scoped ID until you link your Instagram account to a SaveIt account, after which they are associated with that account instead.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Message content is sent to the following processors solely to provide the functionality described above:

  • OpenAI — to interpret message text and thumbnail images
  • Google Places API — to resolve extracted signals into a specific place
  • Supabase — to store your saved places

Data retention and deletion

Raw message data is retained only as long as needed to extract a place and is not kept beyond that for message content. Saved places and your Instagram-scoped ID are retained until you delete them or request account deletion. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

SaveIt is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
